About Southern ADK Help

A free, plain-language directory of help in Warren, Washington and Saratoga Counties, New York: food, housing, health care, transportation, legal help and more.

Why it exists

Getting help often means calling around for hours: who takes my insurance, who is open, who do I even ask? This site gathers what local helpers have learned over years of connecting people to services, and checks it against official sources, so anyone can find the right door faster. It is for people looking for help, their families, and the workers and volunteers who help them.

How listings are kept up to date

  • Every listing shows when it was last checked. "Checked online" means we checked it against the program's own website or an official source. "Confirmed with the program" means we called or emailed them. We aim to re-check food, shelter, rent help, crisis and domestic violence listings every 3 months, and others every year.
  • Insurance tags for "Straight Medicaid" come from New York State's public list of enrolled providers. Other plans are tagged only when the provider says so.
  • See something wrong? Tell us, and we'll fix it.
  • Organizations never pay to be listed, and a listing is not an endorsement.

Who gets listed

We use the same rules for every program, even ones we know. We list a program when:

  • It helps people in Warren, Washington or Saratoga County. Programs farther away count if people from here can use them.
  • It is free or low-cost, takes Medicaid, or fills a need people here often have.
  • We can check its details with the program, its website, or a government source.
  • It is OK with us sharing its details, or it already shares them in public.
  • Addiction treatment programs must be certified by New York State's Office of Addiction Services and Supports (OASAS).
  • It never pays or takes money for sending people to a program.
  • If it sells insurance or earns money when you sign up for a plan, we say so on the listing.

We take a listing down (but keep it on file) when:

  • we can't reach the program after two tries in about a month,
  • it closes, or
  • it gives wrong information about its services, costs or who it helps.

No one can pay for a listing, and we never take gifts or favors for one. Every listing shows the date we last checked it. We try to check each one at least once a year.

Run a program that fits? Email to ask to be listed. We'll check the details with you before adding it.

Help spread the word

Print a one-page flyer with a QR code and tear-off strips for a waiting room, library, food pantry or bulletin board.

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking or analytics. The site never asks for information about the people it serves. The anonymous "what's getting in your way" form collects only a county and the barriers you tick, with no typing. The correction form asks only which listing and what to fix; it never asks for your name or contact information. The "Did something not work?" buttons on a listing send only which listing and which button you tapped. Tapping one also runs Cloudflare's "are you human" check, as our forms do.

Open source

The code behind this site is free and open source (MIT license), so other communities can run their own version. See the code and how to set one up: github.com/J2matlock/community-resource-directory.
